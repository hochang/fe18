Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave — English community research

Compiled 2026-10-31. Scope: English-language guides, fan wikis and forum threads (Serenes Forest, GameFAQs) reachable through web search. Covers difficulty and modes, carry-over, the class system, per-unit builds and 100% recruiting. Game released 2026-09-17 on Switch 2.

Quick take

  • Difficulty: only Normal and Hard, plus a separate Casual (units retreat) / Classic (permadeath) switch. No source I read reports a harder mode than Hard as of late October 2026. Hard can be lowered to Normal at any time at the Shrine of Causality; raising it needs “Descend Again” from the title screen, which replays the Prologue but keeps story progress (Game8, GameTyrant).
  • No New Game+. Instead, “Replay from any chapter” bookmarks plus carry-over between Part I paths: supports, Bless levels, Notable Deeds, Boons of Salvation, map exploration, subquest completion and unlocked classes persist; unit levels, items and recruits do not (RPG Site, allthings.how, Game8).
  • Classes: Beginner → Specialty → Advanced → Master (Part III, Lv 45) → Divine (8 classes, one holder each, Part III Section 4). Eight Advanced classes are route-locked: Dancer (Leda), Blacksmith (Dietrich), Cataphract (Theodora) and Caladrius (Cai) each have a single route; Ranger, Guardian, Dragoon and Troubadour each have two. Unlocks are shared across the save (Game8, GameRant).
  • Top units (multi-source): Sirocco is S in all four tier lists checked. Guzran, Esmeralda, Fabio, Olympia, Mu and Sofia reach S or A+ in at least two. Lists disagree heavily elsewhere (Dante is B in Game8 and FW.uk, S in Keengamer, A in Pocket Tactics).
  • Strongest builds named most often: Dietrich → Swordmaster → The Blade; Leda → Dancer (then The Apsara); Theodora/Eshmel → Sentinel → The Trident; Cai → Orichaldia → The Cavalier; mages → Druid or Wiseman → The Avatar; archers → Bow Adept / Bow Knight; Mu, Nezha and Bertrand → Battlemaster → The Eternal.
  • 100% recruiting: one run cannot do it (community says four runs minimum). Paralogue windows in September and October gate Gaitz, Ursula, Peppe, Sha Lan, Tialla, Diego, Buccar and Fabio; Creek/Nathan and several Part III units need Part I/II prerequisites. Finish each route before starting the next so the shared progress carries over, and recruit the same unit on more than one route only if you plan to train the copies differently and merge them.

Changing difficulty mid-run

  • Lower it (Hard to Normal), any time: talk to the Pale Raven in Dagsion (unlocked after Chapter 4 of any lord route), enter the Obelisk Chamber, use the Shrine of Causality on the second floor and pick “Change Difficulty”. Story progress is not affected (GameTyrant, Game8).
  • Raise it (Normal to Hard): not possible at the Shrine. Use “Descend Again” on the title screen, which replays the Prologue and only affects Prologue actions such as character customization; progress on your routes is kept (Game8). Game8's post-game checklist lists “replay on a higher difficulty” via Descend Again (Game8). The community site adds that it takes about 20 minutes even skipping scenes, keeps Boons of Salvation and other chapter saves, and also lets you change Casual/Classic and character creation (fortunesweave.co.uk) unverified. A GameFAQs thread titled “PSA! You can change the difficulty mid game to hard” exists (GameFAQs) snippet only; replies reportedly say no progress is lost and cutscenes can be skipped.
  • Casual vs Classic after starting: conflict Game8 does not say. GameTyrant says it found no way to change it. The Games Wiki snippet and the Keengamer route article say it can be changed later at the Obelisk Chamber (Games Wiki, Keengamer). The community site says Descend Again can change mode, citing Game8 Japan as unverified. Treat “can switch Casual/Classic” as probable but not confirmed.

Community feel

  • Early Serenes Forest posters: several found it easy even on Hard, one called the difficulty “non existent around chapter 5”, another reported a war-phase spike and said low-attack units are useless on Hard; one poster credited 20 turn rewinds from the start for the ease (thread) snippet only.
  • Kotaku (about 30 hours in) found dungeon clashes low-stakes and the real EXP source (strategic map battles) rare, with training limited to once a week in the main city (Kotaku).
  • The community site author found Part I easy and Part II hard, and suggests spending slack in Part I on class exams and temple service instead of raising difficulty (fortunesweave.co.uk).

2. New Game+ and carry-over

There is no New Game+ and no post-game-only content. After the credits the game returns you to just before the final boss (a “Clear Save”) and you can replay any part (Game8, Game8 post-game, RPG Site).

Replay rules

  • The game saves a bookmark at the start of every chapter. Loading a chapter restores it as first reached, and progress in that chapter and later ones on that path is not included; later bookmarks stay until you replay the chapter before them (RPG Site).
  • When it unlocks: after you clear Part I of any route (Pocket Tactics). allthings.how says you must finish all 12 chapters of the path and pick Chapter 1; the cross-path carry-over only applies if you finish the first path before starting the next (allthings.how, Game8).
  • Replaying from Chapter 1 imports map exploration and subquest completion; replaying from Chapters 2 to 12 imports nothing (fortunesweave.co.uk unverified). Replaying Chapter 12 of a path updates the starting party of that lord's Part II War Arc (RPG Site).
  • Levels and items do carry into the same lord's Part II and III (allthings.how).
  • Renown: RPG Site calls Renown global; one GameFAQs post says Renown and recruited characters reset on a new path while supports and Notable Deeds carry (RPG Site, GameFAQs thread) conflict; Keengamer says it is unclear (Keengamer). Plan as if Renown must be rebuilt.
  • Descend Again replays the Prologue without losing Part II/III progress and unlocks two extra cutscenes (one for finishing a Part II path without skipping, one for progressing Part III) (Game8). Replaying the Prologue on a Clear Save also unlocks two scenes (RPG Site).
  • Other post-game goals Game8 lists: play skipped Part I/II paths, recruit missed units, unlock all Divine classes, finish all Boons of Salvation, replay on a higher difficulty (Game8).

Merge Causality (Part III)

  • At the Shrine of Causality in the Obelisk Chamber, once you reach Part III. Eligible: a unit recruited on more than one Part I path, with each of those paths completed, and recruited again in Part III (TheGamer).
  • Cost: 300 Karma Shards per merge according to Keengamer and the community site (Keengamer, fortunesweave.co.uk unverified); TheGamer says the cost is only shown in-game. Karma Shards are also spent on Boons of Salvation, so budget them.
  • What merges: each stat takes the highest value across versions (no adding, never lowered); higher weapon/magic ranks, extra classes, mastered-class abilities and Combat Arts also transfer. Example from TheGamer: a Theodora-route Seteth who had mastered Cataphract gave the Part III Seteth Cataphract access and Pavise (Keengamer, TheGamer).
  • Developer intent: recruiting the same character on two routes and building them differently makes them stronger when merged (Siliconera, X post quoting Higuchi).
  • Unverified community warnings: merging then restarting a Part III section may lose the merge and shards (fortunesweave.co.uk).

3.5 What sources call the strongest classes and skills

  • Master classes are the default endgame (Part III, Lv 45): Swordmaster for sword users (Astra overshadows Twin Blades), Battlemaster for axe/gauntlet (War Master's Strike), Bow Adept for archers (Sky Hunter), Bow Knight for mounted archers (Hunter's Cross), Orichaldia for cavalry (Fierce Spear), Castle Knight for tanks (Intercept), Druid and Wiseman for pure mages, War Monk for gauntlet-healers (Keengamer, allthings.how, Game8).
  • Advanced-tier mastery skills allthings.how singles out: Warrior's Practiced Art (+10 Hit on arts), Archer's Crescendo (+3 Hit per kill, up to +30 for the map), Rogue's In Shadow (+10 Avo after a kill), Shido's Ever Vigilant, Caladrius's Overtake (+1 Mov per kill, up to +3) and Castle Knight's Intercept. Other masteries it lists: Sniper Marksmanship (+15 bow Hit), Ranger Countershot, Dragoon Besieger, Armored Knight Great Counter (allthings.how).
  • Cai: TheGamer prefers a Caladrius main class (Rider D and Black Magic B needed) built through Diviner and Shaman, keeping Overtake and visiting Orichaldia only for Fierce Spear; Game8 prefers the pure cavalry line to The Cavalier (TheGamer, Game8).
  • Dancer: needs Bow B and Sword B; only one Dancer at a time; Leda is the default first Dancer but Dance competes with her Blaze buffs for the same action. TheGamer and allthings.how suggest Jester as the long-term Dancer and Nezha for The Apsara; pick a unit whose own turn is replaceable and who can survive next to the main attacker. Serenes Forest posters floated Tialla, Ursula and Peppe snippet only (TheGamer, allthings.how, Serenes Forest).
  • Elephant Rider (Part III): +10 HP, +7 Def, -5 Spd class bonus, War-Elephant Boots halve damage taken but the unit cannot avoid attacks, Path growths up at Lv 45+ (HP 30, Def 25, Dex 35); Fextralife's page snippet lists Renown 8 and Riding C (Fextralife). A GameFAQs endgame thread names Theodora or Ninae; another poster used Creek or Nathan; a Serenes Forest poster said an Elephant Rider walled off whole map sections (Game8, GF endgame thread, GF elephant thread, Serenes Forest board; thread not identified) snippet only.
  • Weapon-line caveats: a GameFAQs thread complains the Advanced armored classes lack sword access; replies conflict on whether Shido can use spears, and the Fandom class list shows Shido with sword and spear (GameFAQs, Fandom) snippet only.
  • Pre-release fan tier lists rank Charioteer, Ornius Rider and Cataphract S and Guardian, Wing Soldier, Elephant Rider, Armored Knight A (fireemblemfortunesweave.wiki via fortunesweave.co.uk). They predate release and are low confidence; do not rely on them.
  • Excluded: fortunesweavewiki.wiki's “best class for each character” page (link) contradicts every other source (calls Sofia “Sophia”, Ninae “Flayn”, says Fabio is not recruitable in Part I); not used for builds.

3.6 Broken combos?

No source or thread I could read identifies an outright broken combination, and no verified combat-art exploit surfaced. Synergies that guides call out:

  • Dietrich crit stack: Murderous Intent (+5 Crit attacking first), Ravaging Arts, Ever Vigilant, Astra, The Blade (+10 Sword Crit) and the Hidden Bladerule duel art (Might +10, Crit +100, 10% Hit, no follow-up) (Game8 Dietrich page, FW.uk lord talents).
  • Fabio + Kiroc: Fabio's Dark Calling (Ddg -5 on adjacent foes) makes crits easier; Kiroc's Tyranny (+3 Atk, +10 Crit vs status-afflicted foes) pairs with status weapons (Venin Bow, Poisonbone Chain) and with Fabio (Game8 teams).
  • Hidden Truesnipe (Range 3-5, Hit -20, durability 30) becomes practical in Part II because durability resets after each map; offset the Hit penalty with Peter or Nuzzuo (Game8 combat arts).
  • Dante's Stage Directions (+10 Ddg to allies within 2 spaces) lowers enemy crit chances for the whole squad (Keengamer tier list).
  • Hidden Flowform (+20 Avo, follow-up regardless of AS, half damage) on units with follow-up personal skills such as Diego and Ludia (Game8 combat arts).
  • Prodigy Gamers' accessory guide (dated today) claims “broken” poison and confusion accessory combos; I could not verify item names or numbers and the text is unclear low confidence (link).

5.6 Pitfalls and missables

  • Chapter 12 is the last chance in Part I for Support gains, some cutscenes and older quests (Game8). Do not wait for Chapter 12 to recruit people: a GameFAQs PSA found Lysander and Halvin “Absent” in Chapter 12 despite meeting requirements, and Anatolia's team availability varies by route snippet only (PSA thread, Ch 12 thread). Replies in another thread warn that Jester and Olympia each need three consecutive quests snippet only (deadline thread).
  • Gaitz and Fabio only open after the relevant team is knocked out of the Heroic Games, around Ch 12 (Gaitz after Bertrand's team on Dietrich's route; Fabio after Dietrich's team on Leda's route) (RPG Site, FW.uk).
  • Anatolia's ship loan (Theodora's route): lend the ship by 23:59 on Sept 15, otherwise Anatolia and Sha Lan do not join in the Ch 12 final battle (Game8 via FW.uk).
  • Dagsion arrival dates: Benditz about 11 Apr, Kiroc 4 May, Jasmine 8 May, Alexandra 29 May, Nuzzuo 1 Jul, Inyoni 10 Jul (in-game dates; sources use different D/M orders) (RPG Site, FW.uk). Kiroc on Dietrich's route should be recruited before the Ch 6 main quest (Game8).
  • Answer- and item-gated recruits: Yang Jie (Cheese, Moon, Potatoes); Sha Lan (Saramis, Da Mina, Grounded Ship); Simon (500G coin flip, pick Tails); Ursula (Yes three times); Majide (admit you need him three times); Zarcone (refuse his fee until it drops to 10G, a fourth refusal fails); Goliath (3 Giants' Meat); Ninae (Paradise Fish, Lake Brontes); Mu (3 Glirmosa); Nezha (3 Sandworm Meat); Kiroc (Pure Water); Halvin (10 Dates); Dadao (2 Kothar Gar); Nydine (Bronze Axes); Loretta (3 Iron Swords); Nuzzuo (Iron Bows); Peter (Phantom Ginji) (Keengamer, FW.uk, Game8).
  • Retry: the community site says recruitment cannot be permanently failed and attempts can be retried (some cost gold); Game8 warns “recruitment can fail” if conditions are not met conflict (FW.uk, Game8). Zarcone and Simon are the documented fail-and-retry cases.
  • C supports: each unit's C support is viewable only in its window; missing it blocks the B support and the only fix is replaying from an earlier chapter (FW.uk missables, FW.uk).
  • Part II base change: convoy items not held by a unit become inaccessible and healing items cannot be moved; community reports say the stock returns in Part III (FW.uk) unverified.
  • Dividing the roster: Part I usually deploys about 6, Part II up to 13, and guests fill empty slots; GameFAQs posters suggest roughly 12-13 recruits per route before Part II, recruit early, and note rewards from some activities scale with army size snippet only (GameFAQs thread, balanced-recruits thread). One player regretted prioritising levels over roster size (Part II maps felt thin).
  • Skipping Part II: allowed route by route; a skip reportedly raises every unit's level by 5 (Game8 via FW.uk, unverified). Game8 advises completing or skipping at least one Part II path before using Descend Again after Part III so more units can be recruited (FW.uk FAQ, Game8 missables).

5.8 Recruit the same unit on several routes?

  • Yes, it is allowed: recruiting on one route does not recruit on another, requirements and timing differ per route, and the unit may appear at a different class tier (Game8).
  • Reason to do it: Merge Causality in Part III. Gains are largest when copies are trained differently (a different class line, weapon type or patched weak stat); skip merges that add only +1 Luck or a minor rank unless the unit is central (Keengamer, TheGamer, GameFAQs thread).
  • Reason not to: it costs gold, Support, Renown and chapters each time; OutputLag says borrowing is worth it only to use a favorite on a second path or to merge later, and that Merge Causality means a duplicate is not fully wasted (OutputLag). Merges cost 300 shards each, shared with Boons.
  • The unit must be recruited on more than one Part I path and recruited again in Part III; a unit recruited once has nothing to merge, and some units (Bonaventure, Tobias, Gaitz) only exist on one route (TheGamer, OutputLag).

5.9 A workable plan assembled from the above

This is my synthesis of the sources, not a community-endorsed plan. derived

  1. Start on Cai (fewest missables, easy mounts and materials, mentoring only here, Kindness Quests for Renown). Finish all 12 chapters before switching so map, subquests, supports, Bless levels, deeds, Boons and class unlocks carry over.
  2. On every route, do all paralogues in their windows before Chapter 12, and recruit anyone whose gates are met before Chapter 11 rather than waiting.
  3. Put the date-critical recruits on the routes that can reach them: Gaitz on Dietrich (Sept 17 Bertrand's paralogue); Buccar via Leda or via Diversionary Tactics on Dietrich/Theodora; Sha Lan on Dietrich or Leda (Oct 5-17); Ursula/Peppe on Cai, Theodora or Dietrich; Fabio on Dietrich (or Leda via Sealed-Off Past); Bonaventure and Tobias on Theodora.
  4. Do the four single-route class unlocks across the four routes (Dancer on Leda, Blacksmith on Dietrich, Cataphract on Theodora, Caladrius on Cai) and the second-route options (Ranger, Guardian, Dragoon, Troubadour) where convenient.
  5. Keep Creek's Part I subquest in mind (not on every route), play at least one Dietrich or Leda Part II without skipping, keep guests alive, and do the Part I paralogues for Orchel, Talimun, Anatolia and Bertrand.
  6. After the credits use the Clear Save, Descend Again and the Obelisk Chamber to finish any missed units, Divine classes and Boons.
