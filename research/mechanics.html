Mechanics: classes, difficulty, merging, tiers

Compiled 2026-10-31 from Game8 EN (class list 620256, difficulty 620626, merge 626047, tier list 624024, paralogues 624240, best recruits 624373). Master/Divine class details partly came from a summarised part of the page — marked (unverified).

Difficulty

  • Two difficulties: Normal (weaker enemies) and Hard (higher enemy levels/stats; Blessings cost more Divine Sand).
  • Two modes: Casual (fallen units return next map) and Classic (permadeath).
  • Lower difficulty any time at the Shrine of Causality (Obelisk Chamber). You cannot raise it there.
  • To raise it: Descend Again from the title screen and redo the Prologue. Route progress is kept; only Prologue actions (incl. character customisation) reset.
So an easy-to-hard plan works: play early paths on Normal, then Descend Again on Hard before the later paths.

Merge Causality

  • Obelisk Chamber → Shrine of Causality → Merge Causality. Unlocks after Part III Section 1.
  • Pick the unit, then the path that supplies the main stats and class. Cost: 300 Karma Shards per unit.
  • Keeps the highest stats across paths, and combines skills, combat arts and skill levels from Parts I–II. Training different classes per path can reach S in all skill levels.
  • Without merging, Part I recruits still join in Part III Section 2 by talking to them.
  • CN note: after a merge, never restart the stage or the shards are lost. Game8: Karma Shards are otherwise best spent on Boons of Salvation.
This is why reused units should run a different class on each path: the merge stacks their weapon ranks, and the last (strongest) path should be the "main stats and class" source.
