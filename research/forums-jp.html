1.5 No New Game+

  • There is no separate 2nd playthrough, no New Game+, and no added difficulty such as Lunatic. After clearing Part III, loading the save returns you to just before the Underworld Gate (underworld gate) fights (Lyrah, Balor); all Part III sections stay replayable. G8 post-clearAN NG+
  • The "replay" structure is the four lord routes: one shared save with four route slots. Each route's Part I clear, plus Part II clear or skip, writes a "clear record" that Part III reads. Re-clearing a route updates its record per item, taking the higher value. AN NG+
  • Game8 and Anna's Notebook agree there is no direct NG+; GameWith still files cross-route sharing under replays / carry-over. That is a naming difference, not a contradiction. G8 post-clearGW carry
  • Community asks for Lunatic mode exist; one thread says current balance wouldn't support it and another says Part II needs it. (L) matome diff
  • DLC (Expansion Pass, 3 parts): part 1 (released 2026-10-15) adds one themed weapon per lord route; part 2 (winter) makes the gods Aurora, Smyrnos, Jurah recruitable in Part III (no level growth) plus outfits; part 3 (2027) adds stories for the rivals Bertrand, Talimun, Orchel, Anatolia. No difficulty, route, or NG+ change mentioned. Price per Saiganak snippet: 4,400 yen. Automaton DLCSaiganak DLC

2. Class system

2.1 Structure and promotion gating

  • 60 classes: Basic class 2, Beginner class 5, Specialty class 12, Advanced class 17, Master class 16, Divine classes 8. AN class
  • Promotion needs the target class's exam (Certification Exam) with an exam pass ticket (exam ticket): level, Renown (fame), weapon skill rank, and the class being unlocked. Rough thresholds: Beginner Lv5 / fame 1; Specialty Lv20 / fame 4 / skill about C; Advanced Lv35 / fame 8 / skill about B; Master Lv45 / skill about A. Fame alone does not unlock master classes. AN classG8 classAN FAQ
  • Beginner unlocks through the Certification Exam in story chapter 4 (Theodora route) or 5 (others). Master unlock after Part III §1; Divine classes at §4 start, with Key of the Diadem (5 keys from dungeon chests) to open Temple of the Diadem. G8 classGW divine
  • Master skills and stat-growth bonuses earned in a class are kept after changing class, so finishing a class with a useful master skill is usually worth it. Worth mastering (GameWith): Myrmidon (Precision Strike), Archer (Good Form), Armored Knight (Great Counter), Armored Ornius Rider, Sniper (Marksman), Warrior (Expert Skill), Bardinger (Holy Shield). Lower priority: Light Cavalry, Priest, Shaman, Brigand (skip if heading to Warrior). zatsudan
  • Do not spread skills: pick 2-3 tied to the target class (one weapon to S plus a support skill). RF class
  • Mounts/animals: bond Lv5 gives each animal stat and growth bonuses (about +25% total growth in the examples). Animals are caught on the Cai route from Ch5. G8 classG8 train

4.8 Suggested route plan (my synthesis, not from any source)

  1. Start with Cai (the consensus first route). Do the Cai-available paralogues: Talimun (Ch10), Anna (Ch10), Bertrand (Ch10, 9/17-9/25), Orchel (Ch11, only 10/18-10/20). Unlock Caladrius, Dragoon, Troubadour by training. Spend first shards on Blessings of Salvation (EXP gained, weapon discount).
  2. Next Theodora: Bonaventure, Tobias; do all War Preparations by Ch8 for Cataphract (also Guardian and Dragoon); take the Ch10 boat loan for the Ch12 reinforcements.
  3. Then Leda or Dietrich (sources split). Leda: Dancer, Mu, Sirocco, Buccar, Olympia as free joiners; Ch9 "My Late Sister's Accessory" for Creek/Nathan. Dietrich: Gaitz (9/17), Mikaela, Fabio, challenge letter, all four rival paralogues including Anatolia.
  4. On every route reach Ch12 before ending recruitment; clear Part II at least once on every lord (skip is allowed afterwards) so all four lords join Part III.
  5. For units you want strong in Part III, recruit them on two routes with different builds before each Part II clear; fuse in Part III after §1.

4.9 Recovery rules

  • "Replay Chapter" (menu → system) restores the chapter start: levels, items, skills, scouting, quest rewards and service work revert; support values, bought Blessings of Salvation and existing clear records stay. Best used at the start of a chapter, e.g. eat meals for support, then restart. AN NG+AN FAQ
  • Choosing a past chapter in Obelisk replaces that route's in-progress state; later chapter start points stay until overwritten. The clear record updates only after finishing Ch12. Rewards from an old chapter carry into your late-game data only after you clear through Ch12 again (to refresh Part II start data). AN NG+AN FAQ
  • Missed recruits and paralogues must be recovered by replaying the chapter; clearing the game does not unlock anything. In Part III, "Replay Chapter" restores the section start but rolls growth and items back; losing a battle can be undone with Divine Sand (consumed on Hard, free on Normal). k1 Part3GW gaidenRF irrev
  • In Classic, try the rewind first and replay only if that fails. RF irrev
