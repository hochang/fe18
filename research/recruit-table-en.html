  • Creek — Part II Ch2, only if Part I subquest "My Late Sister's Accessory" was done (not on every path).
  • Nathan — Part II Ch3, Creek must defeat him.
  • Aswan, Tahonia — Part II Ch3 guests; join in Part III only if they survive.
  • Anatolia, Orchel, Talimun, Bertrand — Part III first section; Game8/PT say their paralogue must be cleared.
  • Centurio — clear "Rescue Centurio", talk to him in Dagsion.
  • Klapka — must survive the Mahadu battle; Game8 says Theodora's path must be cleared.
  • Eshmel, Hong Hua, Troy — prologue characters; Hong Hua and Troy auto in Part III (OL/PT).

Shared vs per-path progress (Outputlag)

  • Shared across the save: support levels, unlocked classes, Bless levels, Notable Deeds, Boons of Salvation.
  • Per path: unit levels, items.
  • Same unit on two paths → Shrine of Causality "Merge Causality" combines progress.
  • Part III squads: 8 units each; 2 squads at start, up to 5.
  • Recruitment on a path closes when that path's Part I ends.

Where sources disagree

  • Sha Lan: Game8 Dietrich 10R / Leda 8R; Outputlag says Dietrich is 2R cheaper.
  • Io, Inyoni: Outputlag says "free"; Game8 lists gold costs (800G / 3,000G).
  • Nuzzuo: Outputlag says early on Theodora; Game8 has Theodora latest (Ch9), Cai earliest (Ch6).
  • Kiroc: Outputlag picks Dietrich; Game8 has Leda one chapter earlier at same S1/4R.
  • Benditz, Dante, Halvin, Nydine, Yang Jie: small renown/support differences.
  • Class names: Game8 (Shido, Bardinger, Dreadnought, Caladrius…) vs Outputlag (Orichaldia, Sentinel, Great Knight…) don't match. Game8's used here.
