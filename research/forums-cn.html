Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave (火焰纹章 万缕千丝) — Chinese community research

Compiled 2026-10-31. Scope: Chinese-language guides and forum reposts (GameSky 游民星空, 17173, 18183, 3DM, PlayBlackBox, bilibili video pages, Bahamut 巴哈姆特, 什么值得买, fan wikis). Numbered links such as [1] go straight to the source; the full list is under Sources.

0. How reliable is this page

Read this first.

  • NGA, Baidu Tieba, Zhihu and Xiaoheihe could not be read directly. NGA returns a login wall, Tieba and Zhihu returned HTTP 403, and Tieba/Xiaoheihe searches returned nothing about this game. The nearest thing to NGA content is GameSky's first-playthrough article, which says it is reposted from NGA[12], and PlayBlackBox's article, which is a forum-style player write-up[35].
  • Every page was read through an automatic summarizer, not by me line by line. Treat exact numbers (renown, fragment costs, stat values) as medium confidence unless two sources agree.
  • Chinese names differ between sources (see section 7). Several guides are individual players' opinions, not data.
  • No source gave a formal tier list. The bilibili video titled "SS tier has only one unit" has no readable ranking[49]. The "tiers" in section 4 are my grouping of repeated community opinion.

Key findings (short version)

  • Difficulty: two independent switches. Difficulty is 普通 Normal or 困难 Hard, and mode is 轻松 Casual or 经典 Classic (permadeath). Both can be changed later at the Obelisk Hall's Altar of Causality (因果祭坛), then applied through 再次降临 (Re-descent), which keeps route progress and unit growth[1][2].
  • "NG+": I found no separate New Game+ menu. The community's 二周目 (second run) means repeating Part I/II routes and re-descending. Part III only inherits the first run's warehouse. Later runs buy items back with 因果碎片 (Causal Fragments) at the 依凭圣柜 (Shrine of Causality)[12][13].
  • Classes: about 63 classes in 6 tiers, the top being eight 神将 (God Generals)[37]. The special classes players point to are 卫士 (Guardian) and 锻造师 (Forgemaster) on the Dietrich route, and 舞者/舞娘 (Dancer) on the Leda route.
  • Most-praised units (several independent sources): 迪托利希 (Dietrich), 索绯雅 (also written 索菲亚), 欧露赫露/猫神官 (Orchel), 西洛可 (Sirocco), 卡塔妮雅 (Catania), 谭利穆恩 (Talimun). Part III is described as a hit-rate game, so archers with accuracy bonuses (皮特鲁 Peter, 努佐 Nuzo, 亚斯望) are valued.
  • Route order has no consensus. Easiest-first is Cai first[31]. Collection-first usually puts Cai last, because of animals and mounts[35][12].

Changing difficulty or mode after starting

  • Yes, it is changeable. Advance the main story to the 方尖塔大厅 (Obelisk Hall), interact with the 因果祭坛 (Altar of Causality) and pick 更改难度. To apply a change, save, return to the title screen and choose 再次降临 (Re-descent). Re-descent keeps each route's progress and the units' growth[1][2].
  • Hard to Normal can also be done at the 原因之祭坛 in the deepest part of the Obelisk. That route needs a return through 白鸦的栖木 (White Crow's Perch), which is not available on some schedules[4].
  • Re-descent also lets you change the avatar's appearance and name[8].
  • Classic losses are recoverable by replaying the chapter (menu, 系统, 重玩章节). In-battle rewind uses 天刻白沙[4].

Conflict. NS45 says it could not tell whether difficulty is changeable mid-save[3]. GameSky and 17173 both say it is, with specific steps[1][2]. I follow the latter. Another GameSky item says Re-descent for appearance/name needs a replay from the prologue even though "some progress carries over"[4]. That contradicts the claim that route progress is kept, so verify in-game.

  • "Maddening" is not in the launch game. NS45 only speculates it may arrive in a free update, as in Three Houses. It is unconfirmed[3].
  • How hard is Hard in practice. One 17173 author says that on Hard the 加护 (blessing) system makes clearing easy whatever the roster, while poor unit choices hurt without it. Parts II and III demand much more accuracy[16]. Another player cleared the whole game with no save-scumming, almost no stat potions or combat arts, and says correct strategy beats stats[14]. These are single-player opinions low-medium.
  • There is a single save slot. Manual and auto saves overwrite each other[31], and stat allocation is locked per save[45] low.

2. Second run and carry-over (二周目 / 继承)

I found no dedicated New Game+ mode. The community talks about 周目继承 (carry-over between runs and routes), 再次降临 (Re-descent) and re-playing Part I/II routes. The tables below describe what each source says.

2.2 Part III inheritance, the Shrine and fragments

  • Warehouse. When Part III starts, the four routes' material bags, item bags, mounts and legion units merge, but only the first playthrough's record is used. Items from the second run onward must be bought with 因果碎片 at the 圣柜. Replaying Part III erases fragment purchases and fusions with no refund[12][13].
  • Snapshot timing. The warehouse record is taken when entering Part II. If unhappy with Part I, replay the chapter before Part II. After Part II is cleared the record cannot change. The authors could not confirm the exact moment and advise sorting the warehouse in Part I Chapter 12[13].
  • Fragment sources. Fragments come from 伟业录 entries. Each lord's Part I clear gives 5,000, per a GameWith repost[9]. Part I side stories also give achievements and fragments[13].
  • Fragment uses, in the recommended order. (1) 救世祝福, from Part I and permanent. (2) 因果融合 (Causal Fusion), from Part III. (3) 依凭圣柜 purchases[9]. Several authors call fragments scarce and non-repeatable[12][16].
  • Shrine unlock. The Shrine unlocks after clearing Part II twice with the same character, and brings the second Part II run's items to Part III. The same page is internally inconsistent about whether Part I items also come[9] low-medium.
  • Fusion (因果融合). Unlocks the first time you reach Part III, at the Obelisk's 因果神殿/因果祭坛. For a unit recruited and trained on more than one route and re-recruited in Part III, each stat takes the higher value (no adding), and classes, weapon and skill levels, mastery abilities and combat arts also carry. A weaker result never overwrites a stronger one. The cost is shown in the preview[10]. Cross-route fusion is reported at 300 fragments each[12][11].
  • Same-route fusion is free. Re-clearing Part II on the same route merges that route's records at no cost[13][11].
  • Do not reload after fusing. Replaying a Part III chapter after paying reverts the fusion, and the fragments are lost[16][12].

Conflicts and low-confidence points.

  • One GameSky pitfalls page, as summarized, says same-line fusion costs 300 and the protagonist's is free[8]. The 17173 and Bahamut sources say same-route is free and cross-route is paid. I follow the majority. low
  • Whether the highest level is also kept is unclear. GameSky says the highest level overwrites the others, so over-training on one run wastes later training[8]. The Bahamut author says levels cannot drop but concedes the point is unverified[11].
  • One page says 圣柜 is not mentioned at all in the carry-over article[5]. The Shrine rules above come from the fragment article[9].
  • A 什么值得买 post mentions "lone-wolf" runs that start after the merge with Lv40 upper-class units where the protagonist's death ends the run. It is not corroborated elsewhere[45] low.

2.3 Practical second-run advice from the sources

  • First run: spend time on materials, items and animals. Leave character-building recruits for the second run[12].
  • Do not level units very high on the first run, because merged records take the highest values[8].
  • Plan the final roster first (two eight-unit teams). Concentrate recruiting and support conversations on one route, let the Kai route serve only that roster and its mounts, and fill the warehouse with spare animals[13].
  • Part II: play only one route (the one that recruits the Red and Green Knights, i.e. Leda or Dietrich). Chapters 1 to 3 can be played manually and the rest skipped. Chapters 4 to 6 are back-to-back battles[13][12].
  • Blessing priority: class mastery, sword/lance/fist mastery, dark/white magic mastery, then the cost reductions for the deities 卡拉, 欧若拉 and 马兹[13].

The class count (about 63) comes from a fan wiki that forbids bulk copying, so I only have its summary and some example names medium. Nobody labels a "hidden" tier. The community's "hidden classes" are special advanced classes with quest-style unlocks (section 3.2).

  • Exams. Class changes need qualifying exams at the 上参道 (Upper Approach). You can change class any time from the menu for classes you have qualified for. Unlock timing depends on the protagonist and story progress[4].
  • Tickets. Part I gives few advanced class tickets, so plan to get chosen units into the right intermediate class and meet advanced requirements before Part II. Entering Part II sets all characters to Lv35 and gives five advanced class tickets before each battle[26]. One post says an advanced-class certificate (上级专职证) refreshes each chapter, so collect them to finish promotions before Lv35[35] low.
  • Training tricks. To farm class and skill mastery without changing stat growth, enter a dungeon far below your level, set the unit to the target class with a low-tier weapon of that type, and fight with basic attacks only[26]. Early on only six units deploy, so benched units can change class for the growth bonus and sit in the back to absorb shared dungeon EXP[26].
  • Thief (盗贼). Its passive raises the chance of a perfect exploration result, so spare back-row units can become thieves for gathering[12].
  • Chariot (战车兵). Its class doubles a mount's growth bonus (about +10% per stat). Chariots cannot be dodged[43][12].

3.5 Strong combinations and "broken" setups reported

  • Crit-lifesteal "no-miss" build. Reverse (counter) fighters with high skill plus luck. A 必圣戒指 (crit heals the base hit damage), the 侍道 mastery 残心, crit hits for recovery. Crit is (skill + luck) / 2 and pursuit equals speed. The author's final five are 迪托利希 (skill+luck growth 110), 西洛可 (100, plus 5 HP regen per battle), 卡塔妮雅, 古扎岚 and 爱娜特莉亚[18]. Separately, a player says Dietrich and the cat priestess cleared the finale alone[19].
  • Dancers multiply attackers. One attacker becomes three, and a healer becomes three. 舞神将 plus a 舞娘 gives two units that act again. Each unit can use a Holy Flame combat art only once per turn[14][15].
  • Guardian pair. 乌尔坦德 (Ultand) as a 卫士 becoming 圣枪师 stacks aura bonuses: 欧若拉's 圣刻 (+3 magic defense), the sage mastery (+3 magic defense) and a defensive formation (+2 defense), about +6 magic defense and +2 defense for the group. Pair with another guardian such as Theodora so they heal each other[17].
  • Chariot + mount. 汗血马 (Blood-Sweat Horse) with 战车兵: growth bonus doubled, about STR +25%, SKL +35%, DEF +25%, bow-only and undodgeable[12]. Other combos reported: 赤羽 with 驼兵, 黑羽 with 神鸵兵, and 天马/暗天马 with 雷电剑 (Thunder Sword)[12].
  • Re-move mounts. The author prefers mounts with 再移动 (re-move) and +2 movement, and ended on a black dragon carrying re-move[14]. The catalog lists 黑马 and 黑羽玛吉尼乌斯 with 再移动 and 圣飞马 with 飞行再移动[43].
  • Weapons. Forging is strong: 沙拉曼达 (Salamander, a magic pistol) at +5 reportedly beats most black magic, and wood weapons +9 give +5 speed in Part III. 净化剑 (Purifying Sword) is essential for clearing 冥魔 (Netherworld) barriers, so collect several[14][18][55].
  • Fusion stat patching. Train the same unit on several routes as different roles and merge, taking the best stat of each, for example a physical unit trained as a mage to fill magic defense[27][10].
  • Part III is a hit-rate game. Accuracy matters more than strength or magic. Fliers are plentiful, take no terrain penalty, and carry a built-in bow-hit penalty. Bow-hit bonuses matter: the 狙击手 mastery (+15 in one guide), 亚斯望 (+20), 努佐 (+20 against fliers)[14][15].

Conflict on bow accuracy. 17173 says the 狙击手 mastery gives +15 bow hit[14]. A GameSky skill list puts +15 on 弓术专家 (probably 宗师射手) and +10 on 狙击手 and 弓骑士[44]. That list's class names look machine-translated, so verify in-game.

5.2 Recruiting the same unit on several routes

  • Requirements differ per route. For example, Ultand needs 3 support, 5 renown and a quest on Dietrich, but 3S and 8R on Leda, and joins automatically on Cai[28]. Peter and Tialla vary similarly.
  • Most units can be recruited on several routes, but not all. Route-locked per the GameSky chart: the four lords; Bonaventure and Tobias (Theodora only); Gaitz (Dietrich only); Fabio (Dietrich, or Leda after a side story); Buccar (not on Cai)[28].
  • Recruitment is gated by support (支援 S), renown (名声 R), gold, quests or items. Open recruitment with a first gift. Check each unit's conditions in the character info screen[23][32].
  • Supports are global; renown is not. See section 2.1[5].
  • For Part III fusion, a unit must be recruited and trained on more than one route, and you re-recruit it in Part III without meeting its old conditions again. Vary the class line on each copy rather than making clones[10]. Example from the guide: 西提司 recruited on Kai and Theodora, promoted to 巨重甲骑士 on Theodora, and after merging could promote directly and gained its mastery ability.
  • A well-known trick: restart a long chapter (about 120 turns, Leda Chapter 9) and eat meals to max supports. Buy gifts first. 赛拉弥斯的点心 sold without limit in the capital, plus 小特芙/大特芙 raise about 70% of units[13].
