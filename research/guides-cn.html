Chinese guide notes (万缕千丝)

Compiled 2026-10-31 from GamerSky (incl. an NGA write-up), PlayBlackBox, 17173 and 18183. Recruit numbers match the EN table, so only extra details are recorded. CN pages give no English names; mapping below is by matching numbers.

Route order (guides disagree)

  • NGA (via GamerSky): Leda → Dietrich → Theodora → Cai. Leda/Dietrich are least busy; clear shared side quests there. Dietrich: max supports early, farm materials with thief passive. Theodora: farming/fishing, only route that sails to the southeast island. Cai: animals and mount bonds.
  • PlayBlackBox: Dietrich first, Leda second (covers nearly all hidden classes), Cai last.
  • 18183: Cai → Theodora → Dietrich → Leda (Cai most forgiving).
  • Agreement among the in-depth guides: Cai last; Leda/Dietrich early.

Unit ratings

Dietrich route (PlayBlackBox, scale 夯 > 顶级 > 人上人 > NPC > 拉完了)

  • 夯: Dietrich (sword master), Fabio (best mage, 死神 tome), Esmeralda (General), Alexandra (only recommended flier, 40 Str growth).
  • 顶级: Noctula (brawler), Goliath (wall, magnet skill), Mikaela (chariot), Benditz (chariot).
  • 人上人: Nuzzuo (best archer here), Kiroc.
  • NPC: Ultand (no ranged heal). 拉完了: Yang Jie, Io, Gaitz.
  • Recruit the Bertrand group on Dietrich: Dante (hexer → dark mage), Gaitz + Peppe (chariots), Goliath (pair with Esmeralda as double front line).

Theodora route (PlayBlackBox)

  • Sofia top unit (+1 heal range at Lv20, +2 at Lv35). Theodora top (sacred-flame artillery). Bonaventure good dark mage. Tobias great axe. Lysander weak early, strong later (speed drain; buy Knight Killer in Salamis capital). Lilian benched.
  • Nuzzuo + Inyoni at Ch9 start: "strongest complete forms". Noctula strongest early (~30 Str by end of Part 2). Bertrand (Part 2 guest) carried the hardest maps.
  • Theodora route: fast renown, heavy support pressure — S3/R8+ units usually need meal farming.

Part 3 view (17173)

  • Strong: Mu (learn teleport, then monk), Talimun (best support), Sofia, Sirocco / Guzran / Nezha (melee trio), Lysander, Troy, Nydine (rescue/heal), Nuzzuo, Alexandra (Thunder Sword).
  • Weak: Peter (cavalry dies in Part 3), Esmeralda (should go flying), Dante, Ninae, Centurio, Seteth, Loretta, Jasmine, Noctula (not for Part 3), Bertrand (fragile at Lv40+).
  • Lords: Cai → aim for 神将骑士 (sacred flame weak in Part 3). Dietrich → sword master → sword god-general. Theodora → materials helper, slow. Leda → go archer early or hit rate suffers.

Collection mechanics (NGA)

  • First run is the Part 3 warehouse. Part 1 + Part 2 of the first clear feeds Part 3; all four routes' warehouses merge. Later items go to the 圣柜 (Sacred Cabinet), bought with Causality Shards. Perfect the first run.
  • Unequip mounts and key weapons (Thunder Swords etc.) before the end of Cai's Part 2, ideally before Part 1 Ch12, or they stay bound to that unit.
  • Mounts for Part 3: prepare on Cai's route. Chariot growth bonuses double (example 汗血马 + chariot: +25% Str, +35% Skl, +25% Def). Other combos: red-feather + camel, black-feather + god ostrich, (dark) pegasus + Thunder Sword. Catch at most 1–2 wyverns; Part 3 has better.
  • Causality fusion (因果融合): same-route fusion across playthroughs is free at end of Part 2 (highest stat wins). Cross-route fusion in Part 3 costs 300 shards each; result only lives in that Part 3 run. After fusion, never restart the stage or the shards are lost.
  • Units recruited earlier have more growth room; late recruits come in higher level with worse stats. Merging keeps the highest stats.
  • Side stories in the first playthrough: skipped side-story leaders appear as enemies in Part 3.
  • Part 2: only one route needed — Leda or Dietrich (for Creek + Nathan). Play Ch1–3, skip 4–6.
  • Support farming: ~120-turn chapter (Leda Ch9), eat 3× with the lord, auto-explore to Sunday, restart chapter. Megaira sells 赛拉弥斯的点心 unlimited; 小/大特芙 works for ~70% of units.
  • Open Megaira early: each chapter refreshes one advanced-class certificate so you can promote at Lv35.
  • Some support conversations are gated by other supports — save before reading.
  • Endgame: plan two main squads of 8.
  • NGA suggests leaving heavy-condition recruits (Olympia, Jester, Sirocco, Esmeralda, Seteth, Halvin, Mu, Nezha, Dadao, Goliath, Loretta, Nuzzuo) to a second playthrough unless you love the unit.
  • Mu: raise on Leda (starts there) or Cai (mounts); recruiting elsewhere is a waste.
