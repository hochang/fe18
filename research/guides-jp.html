Recommended route order (game8.jp)

Cai → Theodora → Leda → Dietrich.

  • Cai first: teaches game flow, mount capture, farming.
  • Theodora: legion units (军团兵) and stratagems, party composition.
  • Leda: exploration, performances, secret spots.
  • Dietrich last: combat and combat-art upgrades. Set up the Blessings of Salvation (救世的祝福) first with Causality Shards (因果碎片) earned from clearing each route's Part 1.
  • You can switch lords after Part 1 Ch4 free time unlocks (White Crow's Perch → Obelisk Chamber). The new route must reach Ch4 before switching again.

Negotiation difficulty (game8.jp)

  • Easiest: Yang Jie, Majide, Simon, Zarcone, Sha Lan, Ursula, Fabio.
  • Hardest (extreme, every route): Olympia, Sirocco, Jester.
  • Quiz answers: Yang Jie — cheese, full-moon night, anything. Majide — "Necessary" every time. Ursula — "Yes" to all. Sha Lan — Understood, Salamis, Da Mina, the stranded giant ship. Zarcone — refuse 10000G, 1000G, 100G, then pay 10G. Simon — tails.

Missables and deadlines (in-game dates)

  • Gaitz (Dietrich only): Bertrand side story accepted only on 9/17 in Ch9 (giver: Gabriel); S3/R10; scout from Ch12 on or after 11/4. Hardest unit to get.
  • Sha Lan: Anatolia side story 10/5–10/17 (Dietrich Ch11 / Leda Ch10).
  • Fabio on Leda: Dietrich side story (Leda Ch11, 10/16–11/1), then after losing the main tournament (11/4+).
  • Buccar on Dietrich/Theodora: Leda side story 9/2–9/8.
  • Diego: Orchel side story — Cai only 10/18–10/19 (Ch11); others 10/18–10/27.
  • Ursula: Talimun side story — Cai/Theodora 9/17–9/22 and 10/1–10/12; Dietrich 9/17–9/19 and 10/1–10/12.
  • Peppe: Bertrand side story — Cai/Theodora 9/17–9/25; Dietrich 9/17 only.
  • Tialla off Cai: Cai side story 10/16–10/22.
  • Missed quests can be reset by replaying a chapter from the Obelisk (effect on growth data unverified).

Part 2 / Part 3 joiners (JP view)

  • Creek (齐利科): needs "My Late Sister's Accessory" from Dietrich or Leda route; joins at Part 2 Ch2–3. Skipping the war arc loses him. Rated B.
  • Nathan (内森): defeat with Creek in Part 2 Ch3. Rated D.
  • Orchel (S), Talimun (A), Anatolia (A), Bertrand: auto-join in Part 3 if their side story was cleared.
  • Centurio: clear "Rescue request from Centurio", talk to him in Dagsion. Rated C.
  • Klapka: Division 6, must survive Division 5. game8 tested: a save with only Cai cleared did not get him. Clear Theodora's route.
  • Aswan, Tahonia: auto at start of Division 5.
  • Savior, Hong Hua (红花), Troy (特洛伊亚): prologue units, usable from Part 3.
One Dietrich run can hit all four Part 3 side stories (Bertrand 9/17, Talimun, Anatolia, Orchel), but the windows are tight.
