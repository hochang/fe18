Supports and paired endings

Pairs from Game8's paired-endings list (work in progress, read 2026-10-31). The pairing below is computed by the generator from the current roster, so it updates when the plan changes.

How it works

  • No marriage: there are no S supports, no marriage system and no child units (Game8).
  • Paired endings: a character's ending pairs them with whoever they share the most support points with, but only if that pair has unlocked and watched its A support. Some endings are romantic (marriage, proposal, children), some vague, some platonic.
  • Eshmel: after the final battle you pick Eshmel's partner from anyone at Support Level 5. 48 units qualify; Part II/III joiners (except Hong Hua) and Seteth, Kiroc and Nuzzuo can't. No source mentions a gender requirement.
  • Combat: units with higher support buff each other when fighting side by side; the bonus grows with support level (Game8). No source gives the numbers. That's why the pairing below prefers partners who share a squad or path.
  • Lords need their own Part I path completed to get an ending.

14 is the most possible: no unit is in two pairs, and the 22 units listed further down have Eshmel as their only ending partner, while Eshmel can take one. Ties go to partners who fight together (same Part III squad, then same Part I path), then romantic endings.

Raising support quickly

  • Meals at an inn (three units at a time) are half price on Sundays and holidays. Gifts and meals also work on units not yet recruited.
  • Restarting a chapter keeps support points: on a long chapter, eat the three meals, auto-activity to the next Sunday, repeat, then restart (Game8, NGA).
  • Fight side by side, heal or support with arts, and use Link attacks in dungeon clashes.
  • Gifts many units love: Tales of Adventure, Strategy Manuscript, History of a Master, Portrait of Yu Phas, Volcano Ghosh. A gift on a unit's birthday always gets the best reaction.
  • Bird Time raises Eshmel's support with everyone.
  • Pairs on different paths: supports are shared across the save, so build them with meals and gifts on whichever path both are around.
  • Don't max a unit's support with someone else past their partner's, or the ending goes to the other unit.
