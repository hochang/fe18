Masteries and growth rates

Masteries: Game8 class and ability pages. Growths: Keengamer (2026-09-28). Final growth = character growth + class modifier, rolled per stat each level.

How masteries and levelling fit together

  • Fully mastering a class gives its mastery ability for good: you can equip it in any later class (Game8, allthings.how). Merge Causality in Part III carries every copy's masteries to the merged unit.
  • Every level uses the growth modifiers of the class you're in, so spend real levels in the class whose growths fit the unit's role. That's the Level in line on each build card.
  • Masteries from other classes (Farm only on the cards) are earned in a dungeon far below your level: switch into the class, equip a weak weapon of its type and use basic attacks, not one-hit kills. Class experience rises while you gain few levels (CN: NTGame, NGA).
  • Early copies on Normal paths are the cheapest place to bank extra masteries; they flow into the final copy at the Part III merge.
  • Equip in this order (Game8): personal Lv20/Lv35 abilities, the class's own mastery, off-class masteries, then weapon-rank abilities like Swordfaire. Mounted units can't use infantry-only abilities and vice versa. The ability slot count isn't confirmed by any source.
