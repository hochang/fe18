Path 1: Cai (凯伊)

Difficulty: Normal · Casual

Mounts and animal capture. The most forgiving route (18183) and Game8 JP's suggested first route.

Easiest start. Its weak-but-required classes (Light Cavalry, Rogue, Pugilist, Soldier, Armored Knight) and low-tier recruits (Nydine, Peppe, Majide, Dadao) are parked here.

Checklist

  • Class unlocks: 'Special Request from Pop' / Castor's training Lv7 at Renown 7 (Caladrius); 'Special Request from Mom' / Aurora's training Lv8 at Renown 8 (Dragoon); 'White-Maned Demon's Request' / Bertrand's training Lv10 at Renown 10 (Troubadour).
  • Scouting closes at Part I Ch12 (before the Ch12 main battle) on every path.
  • Bertrand's paralogue, Ch10, 9/17–9/25 — required for Peppe.
  • Anna's paralogue, Ch10, 9/24–10/5 — Orichalcum and Anna's shop at Dagsion's Summit.
  • Capture and bond mounts for Part III, especially the Ferghanan Horse (Canto). Raise bonds with the riders you'll keep.
  • Unequip mounts and key weapons before Part I Ch12 if you plan to hand them to other units in Part III (CN/NGA).
  • Don't recruit Noctula here: recruiting her early on Cai can lock 'Ursa's Right Claw' (Game8).
  • Halvin is cheap here on paper but Dates are hard to farm on Cai — he's on Theodora instead.
  • Basic classes (Commoner, Noble; CN wiki, no requirement): if nobody on this path starts in one, switch a benched unit into each for one easy map so both get used.

Recruit and bond walkthrough

  • Recruit support is with Cai. Supports are saved globally, never drop, and carry into Part III.
  • Give each target one cheap gift first: it forms the bond, which unlocks meals and the Recruit option. Gifts and meals also work on units you haven't recruited.
  • Inn meals are the main source of support: up to 3 meals a week, each with Cai plus 2 guests. The menu resets on Sunday.
  • Meal seats are the bottleneck on this path: four recruits need Support 3 (Ninae, Seteth, Dadao, Mu). Don't spend seats on anyone else until they're done.
  • Scouting closes before the Ch12 main battle (11/27).
  • Chapter restarts keep support points: on a long chapter, eat three meals, auto-activity to the next Sunday, repeat, then restart.

Ch1–2 no free time

  • Tialla and Peter join in Ch1.
  • Ch2: chasing the thief opens the Galen Fortress Ruins (a beast-hunting bow in a chest). Castor gives 1,500G on reaching Dagsion. Keep the gold.

Ch3 32 turns · about 2/20–2/28 · expected Renown 2–3

  • Guzran: gift a Mature Ghosh, clear 'Lost Hair Clip' (Niraga Mine, +100 Renown), then recruit him.
  • Bond gifts: one cheap gift each to Ninae, Seteth, Nydine and Peppe.
  • Meals: start with Cai + Ninae + Seteth, the two earliest Support 3 targets. 2/25 is a half-price meal day.
  • Shopping: 2 Bronze Axes at the Dagsion armory for Nydine.
  • Renown: clear the three Cai-only kindness quests (Lost Hair Clip, Furious Vengeance, The Fabled Sunset) and both small side quests, about 380.
  • Don't recruit Noctula here: an early recruit on Cai can lock 'Ursa's Right Claw'.

Ch4 24 turns · 3/1–3/7 · expected Renown 3–4

  • Mu: gift to bond. She loves Candy Crystals, History of a Master and Portrait of Yu Phas.
  • Meals: keep going with Ninae and Seteth.
  • Renown: Ch4's quests give about 350.
  • Glirmosa: if you spot the plant merchant, buy one now so you can farm the rest from Ch6.

Ch5 104 turns · 3/15–4/10 · expected Renown 4–5

  • Bond gifts: Majide and Dadao.
  • Majide: recruit as soon as you're at Support 2 / Renown 5.
  • Meals: rotate the six weekly seats across Ninae, Seteth, Dadao and Mu, plus Majide until he's at Support 2. Cover gaps with loved gifts (Strategy Manuscript and Tales of Adventure work for Ninae; Poems of the Greats for Dadao).
  • Items: fish 2 Kothar Gar at Kothar Lake for Dadao.
  • Renown: about 740, the biggest so far, including capturing 2 wild Ornius (+200).

Ch6 120 turns · 4/20–5/20 · expected Renown 6

  • Ultand joins.
  • Recruit at Renown 6: Nydine (2 Bronze Axes), and Majide if you haven't yet.
  • Ninae and Seteth: once each hits Support 3, fish the Paradise Fish and do Seteth's Request. Both are in the far south, so combine the trip, or wait for the Ch7 carriage.
  • Glirmosa: use the farming tutorial to plant it.
  • Renown: about 670.

Ch7 120 turns · 6/9–7/9 · expected Renown about 7 (the cap around here)

  • Carriage: clear the kindness quest 'Something Not in the Capital' (a special axle at Megaera, +130). The shared carriage makes the Lake Brontes and Mount Ouranos trips much shorter.
  • Recruit: Dadao (2 Kothar Gar), plus Ninae and Seteth if you haven't yet.
  • Castor's mentoring (Renown 7): deliver 2 Angelica at Star-Born Garden to unlock Caladrius, Cai's planned class.
  • Renown: about 750.

Ch8 172 turns (longest) · 7/20–9/1 · expected Renown 8

  • Mu: use the restart trick to finish her Support 3 if she's still short.
  • Spare meal seats now go to bonds outside the recruit list — see 'Extra bonds' below.
  • Aurora's mentoring (Renown 8): defeat the temple soldiers at Fortuna Temple to unlock Dragoon, which Seteth needs.
  • Renown: about 640.

Ch9 32 turns · 9/3–9/11 · expected Renown 8–9

  • Clear Ch9's quests (about 280 Renown) to reach Renown 9.
  • Make sure you have 3 Glirmosa.

Ch10 120 turns · from 9/17 · expected Renown 9–10

  • Peppe: be in Dagsion on 9/17 and talk to Gabriel to start Bertrand's paralogue (+150 Renown), then recruit Peppe at Support 1 / Renown 9. Missing this window loses Peppe on this path.
  • Mu: recruit at Support 3 / Renown 9 with the 3 Glirmosa.
  • Optional: Talimun's paralogue (9/18–9/22 or 10/1–10/10) and Anna's (9/24–10/5; opens Anna's shop) for Renown and completion.
  • Bertrand's mentoring (Renown 10): unlocks Troubadour, Ultand's planned class.

Ch11 28 turns · about 10/16–10/22 · expected Renown 10

  • Optional Orchel's paralogue (10/18–10/20) for Renown. Pick up anyone you've missed.

Ch12 92 turns · until 11/27 · expected Renown 10+

  • Last chance to recruit anyone you've missed: scouting ends at the main battle.

Extra bonds (from Ch8, once recruiting is done)

Spare meal seats are worth spending only on these. Raising Cai's support with a unit doesn't help recruit them on another lord's path: that checks support with that path's lord.

  • Mu + Peter (romantic ending; both are here): get them to A support and watch the conversation. Keep it above Mu–Leda, Mu–Sirocco.
  • Cai + Tialla (romantic ending; both are here): get them to A support and watch the conversation. Keep it above Cai–Theodora.
  • Goliath + Ultand (romantic ending; only one of them is on this path; raise it with meals and gifts): get them to A support and watch the conversation. Keep it above Goliath–Dante, Goliath–Loretta.
  • Loretta + Seteth (platonic or vague ending; only one of them is on this path; raise it with meals and gifts): get them to A support and watch the conversation. Keep it above Loretta–Goliath.
  • Diego + Ninae (platonic or vague ending; only one of them is on this path; raise it with meals and gifts): get them to A support and watch the conversation. Keep it above Diego–Esmeralda.
  • Part III squad-mates (C · Cavalier): Buccar, Loretta, Lysander, Lilian fight next to Cai in Part III but aren't recruited on this path. A few meals with Cai now give side-by-side support buffs later.
  • Three-person meals: inviting both partners of a pair together may also raise support between the two guests. That isn't confirmed, so check the support screen after one meal before relying on it.
  • Don't overshoot: a unit's ending goes to whoever they share the most support points with.
