Path 1: Cai (凯伊)

Difficulty: Normal · Casual

Mounts and animal capture. The most forgiving route (18183) and Game8 JP's suggested first route.

Easiest start. Its weak-but-required classes (Light Cavalry, Rogue, Pugilist, Soldier, Armored Knight) and low-tier recruits (Nydine, Peppe, Majide, Dadao) are parked here.

Checklist

  • Class unlocks: 'Special Request from Pop' / Castor's training Lv7 at Renown 7 (Caladrius); 'Special Request from Mom' / Aurora's training Lv8 at Renown 8 (Dragoon); 'White-Maned Demon's Request' / Bertrand's training Lv10 at Renown 10 (Troubadour).
  • Scouting closes at Part I Ch12 (before the Ch12 main battle) on every path.
  • Bertrand's paralogue, Ch10, 9/17–9/25 — required for Peppe.
  • Anna's paralogue, Ch10, 9/24–10/5 — Orichalcum and Anna's shop at Dagsion's Summit.
  • Capture and bond mounts for Part III, especially the Ferghanan Horse (Canto). Raise bonds with the riders you'll keep.
  • Unequip mounts and key weapons before Part I Ch12 if you plan to hand them to other units in Part III (CN/NGA).
  • Don't recruit Noctula here: recruiting her early on Cai can lock 'Ursa's Right Claw' (Game8).
  • Halvin is cheap here on paper but Dates are hard to farm on Cai — he's on Theodora instead.
  • Basic classes (Commoner, Noble; CN wiki, no requirement): if nobody on this path starts in one, switch a benched unit into each for one easy map so both get used.
