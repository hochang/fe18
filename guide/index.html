Fortune's Weave — 100% team plan

火焰纹章 万缕千丝 · built 2026-10-31 from English, Japanese-site and Chinese guides (see research).

Rules this plan follows

  • Every one of the 63 units is used at least once (46 Part I recruits on the four paths, 2 Part II joiners, 11 Part III-only units, 4 lords).
  • Paths go easiest → hardest. Paths 1–2 on Normal; then Descend Again to Hard for paths 3–4, Part II and Part III.
  • Units used on two paths get a different build each time. The early copy runs an off-meta class; the later, harder path runs Game8's recommended line and becomes the Merge Causality source.
  • Every class is used at least once; weak or utility classes (Light Cavalry, Rogue, Pugilist, Soldier, Blacksmith) sit on the Normal paths.

When to promote

  • Default: promote as soon as the exam is a sure pass. Changing class keeps your level and stats, and every level-up uses the new class's growth bonuses. Levels spent in a weaker class are growth you don't get back (allthings.how, CN guides).
  • Exception 1, mastery skills: a fully mastered class gives its skill as a permanent passive you can equip in every later class. If the skill matters for the build, master the class before leaving (allthings.how). Examples: Myrmidon and Shido for Dietrich's crit build, Sniper's bow-hit bonus for Part III archers, Dreadnought's +10 hit when attacked.
  • Farm mastery, not levels: enter a dungeon far below your level, switch the unit into the class, equip a weak weapon of that type and use basic attacks only. Class and weapon EXP rise while stats barely change (CN: NTGame, NGA).
  • Exception 2, growth classes: Charioteer and Elephant Rider boost growths (can't dodge). Sit in them for a while to fix a unit's stats, then move on (Game8).
  • Licences: one exam licence per attempt, used up even on a fail, so only sit an exam at 100% pass chance. Part I gives few Advanced certificates (Megaira refreshes one per chapter), so promote your main units to Advanced in Part I and get the rest to Specialty with their Advanced skill ranks pre-farmed. Part II sets everyone to Lv35 and hands out five Advanced tickets before each battle (GamerSky), so the bench promotes there.
  • Master classes only open in Part III (gods' requests). Levelling past 45 in a good Advanced class while you wait isn't a loss (Game8: use Shido until Swordmaster unlocks).
  • The Lv20 / Lv35 marks on the build cards are the earliest sensible promotion points, not targets to wait for.
  • Each build card now shows where to level (growths per class), which masteries to keep, which to farm only, and an equip order. Full mastery and growth tables: Masteries and growth rates.

Caveats

  • Recruit numbers come from Game8 EN/JP, GamerSky and Pocket Tactics (Sept–Oct 2026). A 10/15 patch may have changed some; check in game.
  • Most EN guides suggest Cai → Theodora → Dietrich → Leda. This plan swaps Dietrich and Theodora so Blacksmith (Dietrich-only, weakest class) lands on a Normal path and the strongest lord eases the second run; Theodora's defensive kit is the gentler Hard opener.
  • Game8 doesn't list requirements for Soldier, Pugilist or Blacksmith; their placement follows each unit's boons.
  • Master unlock temples marked "unverified" came from a summarised part of Game8's class page.
  • Deployment limits per map aren't confirmed, so rosters of 13–15 assume some rotation.
