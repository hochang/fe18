Path 2: Dietrich (迪托利希)

Difficulty: Normal · Casual

Combat Arts. Dietrich is the strongest lord and the route needs little upkeep, so it doubles as the support-farming route (NGA).

Still on Normal. Unlocks Blacksmith and Ranger here, so the weak one (Blacksmith) is used on a Normal path. Holds the strict one-day Gaitz window.

Checklist

  • Leda's paralogue, Ch9, accept 9/2–9/8 (starts 9/9) — Silver Shield.
  • Theodora's paralogue, Ch9, 9/3–9/15 — Pilum.
  • Bertrand's paralogue, Ch9, 9/17 only (giver: Gabriel) — required for Gaitz and Peppe on this path, and Bertrand in Part III. Be in Dagsion on 9/17.
  • Talimun's paralogue, Ch9, 9/18–9/19 or 10/1–10/12 — required for Ursula, and Talimun in Part III.
  • Cai's paralogue, Ch11, accept 10/16–10/22 (starts 10/23) — Gem Box.
  • Class unlocks: Renown 6 challenge letter vs Il-Ira (Guardian); Renown 10 challenge vs Okō (Ranger); enhance Combat Arts 10+ times at the Smyrnos temple, then talk to the receptionist (Blacksmith — about 400 resonance stones and an advanced exam ticket).
  • Subquest 'My Late Sister's Accessory' — backup for Creek (also done on Leda).
  • Gaitz: scout from Ch12, on or after 11/4, at Support 3 / Renown 10.
  • Max supports early here, including with Cai and Theodora (NGA). Supports are shared across the save.
